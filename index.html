Juicy Slice — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Juicy Slice ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

  • No account, no name, no email. The app works fully offline and never asks for personal information.
  • Scores and settings (best score per game mode) are stored only on your own device. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and app interaction data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where consent applies. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

  • On Android the app declares the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions, used only to load ads.
  • In regions where consent is required (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent message is shown before personalized ads (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice anytime from the device settings (reset advertising ID).

Children

The app is a general-audience arcade game and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Open-source attribution

Juicy Slice is based on an MIT-licensed open-source game. The full copyright and license notices are kept in the app's LICENSE and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES files and on the in-app licenses page.
